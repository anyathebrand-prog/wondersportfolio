FAQs

Answers before we start.

How fast can we start?

Usually within two to three weeks. For launches with a hard date, tell me early and I'll tell you honestly whether it's doable.

Do you only do strategy, or also make the content?

Both. Some clients hire me for the thinking, some for the making, most for the whole arc from brief to final cut.

Do you work with in-house teams?

Often. I can lead the team, plug into it, or train it so the work keeps going after I leave.

What does a project cost?

Strategy sprints start around four weeks; campaigns and retainers are scoped per brief. Send a note and I'll share ranges.
